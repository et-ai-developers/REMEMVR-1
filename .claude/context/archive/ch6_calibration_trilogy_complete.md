### Overview

**CALIBRATION TRILOGY COMPLETE:**
All three calibration metrics show metacognitive deterioration pattern over the retention interval.

### RQ 6.2.1: Calibration Magnitude Worsens

**Hypothesis:** Calibration trajectory shifts from underconfidence to overconfidence

**Finding:** **SUPPORTED** (p_LRT=0.004)

**Key Results:**
- Trajectory shifts from underconfidence (-0.116) to overconfidence (+0.111)
- Zero-crossing occurs between Days 1-3
- Effect statistically significant

**Interpretation:** Person-level calibration (theta difference) deteriorates over time as metacognitive accuracy degrades.

### RQ 6.2.2: Overconfidence Proportion Increases

**Hypothesis:** Proportion of overconfident participants increases over time

**Finding:** **TREND ONLY** (p=0.230, non-significant)

**Key Results:**
- Descriptive increase: +10% (from 41% to 51%)
- Logistic trend test non-significant after Bonferroni
- Classification scheme: ε=0.1 threshold (9% well-calibrated)

**Interpretation:** Calibration deterioration is gradual shift in DEGREE, not discrete category flip. Complements RQ 6.2.1 continuous trajectory finding.

### RQ 6.2.3: Resolution Discrimination Declines

**Hypothesis:** Metacognitive resolution (Goodman-Kruskal gamma) declines over retention interval

**Finding:** **SUPPORTED** (p=0.011)

**Key Results:**
- Mean gamma: 0.729 (Day 0) → 0.662 (Day 6)
- 9.1% decrease over 6 days
- All timepoints exceed γ > 0.50 threshold (acceptable discrimination maintained)
- Effect statistically significant

**Interpretation:** Discrimination ability (gamma) declines as signal-to-noise ratio decreases. Despite decline, participants retain acceptable ability to distinguish remembered from forgotten items.

### Theoretical Integration

**UNIFIED PATTERN:** All three metacognitive dimensions deteriorate over time

1. **Absolute Calibration (RQ 6.2.1):** Person-level bias shifts from underconfidence to overconfidence
2. **Categorical Calibration (RQ 6.2.2):** Proportion overconfident increases descriptively (trend)
3. **Relative Resolution (RQ 6.2.3):** Discrimination ability declines significantly

**Dual-Process Hypothesis:**
- Both absolute (calibration) and relative (resolution) metacognition deteriorate
- Metacognitive discrimination degrades as memory fades
- Signal-to-noise ratio decreases over time → harder to distinguish remembered from forgotten
- Supports unified metacognitive deterioration framework

**Consistency Across Metrics:**
- Calibration magnitude: p=0.004 (SIGNIFICANT)
- Calibration proportion: p=0.230 (trend, +10%)
- Resolution discrimination: p=0.011 (SIGNIFICANT)
