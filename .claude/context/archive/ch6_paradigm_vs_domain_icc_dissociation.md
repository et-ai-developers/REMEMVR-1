## Critical Dissociation Pattern

**Domain ICC (RQ 6.3.4):** TRAIT-LIKE individual differences
- What/Where domains: ICC_slope = 0.59 (SUBSTANTIAL)
- When domain: ICC_slope = 0.00 (UNIVERSAL decline)
- **Interpretation:** Content domain creates stable individual differences in forgetting rate

**Paradigm ICC (RQ 6.4.4):** STATE-LIKE fluctuations
- All paradigms: ICC_slope < 0.06 (NEGLIGIBLE-SMALL)
- ICR (Cued Recall): 0.055 (highest but still state-like)
- IFR (Free Recall): 0.046
- IRE (Recognition): 0.038
- **Interpretation:** Retrieval method does NOT create stable individual differences

---

## Theoretical Significance

**What You Remember > How You Retrieve It**

This dissociation reveals a fundamental distinction:
1. **Domain content (WHAT)** drives trait-like individual differences (ICC=0.59)
2. **Retrieval paradigm (HOW)** shows only state-like variability (ICC<0.06)

**Implications:**
- Forgetting rate individual differences are content-specific, not task-specific
- Retrieval support level (free/cued/recognition) doesn't reveal stable traits
- Domain-specific memory profiles exist; paradigm-specific profiles do NOT
- Researchers should stratify by domain, not paradigm, when studying individual differences
