### Methodological Lessons

1. **Ordinal vs Binary Measurement:**
   - 5-level confidence reveals ~60× more trait variance than binary accuracy
   - Critical for detecting individual differences in What/Where domains
   - Measurement precision matters for trait assessment

2. **Crossover Effects Require Trajectory Analysis:**
   - Post-hoc contrasts can be non-significant even with strong interaction
   - Crossover effects cancel when averaged across timepoints
   - Must examine full trajectory patterns

3. **Domain Stratification Essential:**
   - General confidence analysis (6.1.X) masks domain-specific patterns
   - What/Where vs When dissociation only visible with stratified analysis
   - Domain should be tested as moderator in metacognition research
