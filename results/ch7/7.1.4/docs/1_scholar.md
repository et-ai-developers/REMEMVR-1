#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Overly Optimistic Residual Variance Prediction**
- **Location:** Section 3: Hypothesis - Primary Hypothesis
- **Claim Made:** "Substantial residual (>50%) should remain unexplained after accounting for all available predictors"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** Recent hierarchical regression studies in cognitive assessment show most incremental validity effects are small (ΔR² < 0.10), suggesting >50% residual may be unrealistically high
- **Counterevidence:** 2020 study found specific cognitive abilities beyond general intelligence showed negligible incremental validity (average ΔR² = 0.06) in most populations, with only high-IQ groups showing larger effects
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge that traditional cognitive measures often show small incremental effects, but argue REMEMVR represents fundamentally different construct (naturalistic vs. artificial encoding) that should show larger residual variance. Revise prediction to be more conservative (>40% residual) while maintaining theoretical rationale."

**2. Transfer-Appropriate Processing Oversimplified**
- **Location:** Section 2: Theoretical Background - Transfer-Appropriate Processing
- **Claim Made:** "REMEMVR's naturalistic VR encoding may engage different processes than traditional verbal/visuospatial tests"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** Recent VR research shows mixed evidence for transfer-appropriate processing, with some studies finding limited transfer between VR and real-world contexts
- **Counterevidence:** 2024 study found "contextual matching between learning and testing enhances memory retrieval in some cases, but surprisingly found a reversed reinstatement effect" in VR contexts
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge complexity of transfer-appropriate processing in VR contexts while maintaining that multimodal integration during VR encoding creates different memory traces than traditional unimodal tests."

#### Omission Errors (Missing Context or Claims)

**1. No Discussion of Practice Effects Across 4 Test Sessions**
- **Missing Content:** Concept.md doesn't acknowledge that participants complete REMEMVR testing 4 times (Days 0, 1, 3, 6)
- **Why It Matters:** Practice effects could confound residual variance interpretation - improvements from familiarity might mask memory decay or inflate theta score reliability
- **Supporting Literature:** VR memory research shows practice effects can occur even across multi-day gaps, potentially affecting longitudinal validity
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you ensure residual variance reflects true ecological validity gap rather than practice-related measurement artifacts?"
- **Strength:** CRITICAL
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 6: Analysis Approach - acknowledge that mean theta across 4 sessions controls for practice effects by averaging across learning curve, and that IRT theta estimation accounts for item-level practice effects"

**2. No Consideration of VR-Specific Dropout Bias**
- **Missing Content:** No acknowledgment of potential dropout bias due to VR simulator sickness or technology anxiety
- **Why It Matters:** If certain populations (older adults, those with vestibular issues) drop out preferentially, residual variance patterns could be biased
- **Supporting Literature:** 2024 systematic review found mean VR dropout rate of 15.6% with significant individual differences in cybersickness susceptibility
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "Could differential dropout rates across age groups or cognitive levels bias the incremental validity findings?"
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 7: Analysis Approach - acknowledge VR dropout considerations, report actual dropout rates in REMEMVR study, and include sensitivity analyses if dropout is non-random"

**3. Insufficient Discussion of Measurement Error vs. True Residual**
- **Missing Content:** Limited detail on separating measurement error from true incremental validity in residual variance
- **Why It Matters:** High residual variance could reflect measurement error rather than true ecological validity gap
- **Supporting Literature:** IRT literature emphasizes importance of theta standard errors in interpreting ability estimates and model residuals
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you distinguish measurement error from meaningful incremental validity in the residual variance?"
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 6: Analysis Approach - describe use of IRT theta standard errors to estimate measurement error component and calculate true residual variance after removing measurement error"
