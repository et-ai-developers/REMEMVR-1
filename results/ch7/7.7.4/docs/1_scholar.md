#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Overstated VR Ecological Validity Claims**
- **Location:** Section 2: Theoretical Background - Ecological validity discussion
- **Claim Made:** "VR environments may better simulate everyday memory demands"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** This claim oversimplifies VR ecological validity - while VR may be more naturalistic than traditional tests, it still represents an artificial environment with unique confounds
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge VR limitations while maintaining that REMEMVR may have higher ecological validity than traditional list-learning tasks. Specify that 'better' is relative, not absolute ecological validity."

**2. Assumption of Practice Effect Independence**
- **Location:** Section 4: Analysis Approach - Use of REMEMVR theta scores
- **Claim Made:** Uses omnibus REMEMVR theta scores without acknowledging potential practice effects
- **Scholarly Criticism:** Participants complete REMEMVR across 4 sessions (Days 0, 1, 3, 6), potentially confounding "ability" with practice effects
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge that IRT theta scores help separate ability from practice effects, and that Day 0 performance (used in classification) minimizes practice effects since it's the first assessment."
