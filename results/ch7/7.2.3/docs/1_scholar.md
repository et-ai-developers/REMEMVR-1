#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Overgeneralized Cognitive Reserve Predictions**
- **Location:** Theoretical Background - cognitive reserve theory application
- **Claim Made:** "Tests may show stronger prediction in older adults who rely more on crystallized abilities"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** This conflates cognitive reserve (compensatory processing) with crystallized vs. fluid intelligence distinctions
- **Counterevidence:** Cognitive reserve theory doesn't specifically predict stronger test-performance correlations, but rather alternative processing strategies
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Clarify that cognitive reserve predicts compensatory strategy use, which may manifest as stronger ability-performance correlations when environmental demands exceed typical processing capacity"
