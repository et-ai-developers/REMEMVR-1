SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
============================================================

The absence of significant Age x Test interactions indicates that
cognitive tests predict REMEMVR performance equally well across the
adult lifespan. This finding:

1. SUPPORTS the VR Scaffolding Hypothesis:
   - VR environments provide environmental support that equalizes
     predictive utility of cognitive tests across ages
   - Older adults do not rely more heavily on cognitive abilities
     to compensate for age-related decline in VR contexts

2. DOES NOT SUPPORT Cognitive Reserve Theory predictions:
   - No evidence that high-ability older adults use compensatory
     strategies more than younger adults in VR memory tasks
   - Cognitive test prediction is age-invariant

3. CLINICAL IMPLICATIONS:
   - VR-based memory assessments may provide age-fair evaluation
   - Cognitive test norms may apply consistently across ages in VR

Since no interactions reached significance, simple slopes analysis
at different age levels is not warranted. The main effects of
cognitive tests can be interpreted as applying uniformly across
the age range of 20-70 years.
