#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Overly Simplistic View of Calibration-Intelligence Relationship**
- **Location:** Section 3: Hypothesis - Primary Hypothesis paragraph
- **Claim Made:** "RPM (fluid intelligence) will predict calibration quality more strongly than RAVLT or BVMT"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** This assumes a direct linear relationship between fluid intelligence and metacognitive monitoring, but calibration quality might be influenced by domain-specific metacognitive knowledge that could favor memory-specific tests
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge that calibration involves both domain-general monitoring (RPM) and domain-specific metacognitive knowledge (memory tests), but predict domain-general processes dominate given that calibration aggregates across all memory domains"

---

#### Omission Errors (Missing Context or Claims)

**1. No Discussion of Confidence Scale Usage Individual Differences**
- **Missing Content:** Concept doesn't address that participants may use the 5-point confidence scale differently (response bias)
- **Why It Matters:** Individual differences in scale usage could confound calibration measures - some participants consistently use extreme ratings while others stick to middle ratings
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you distinguish genuine calibration differences from confidence scale usage patterns?"
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 4: Analysis Approach - mention that confidence ratings were 'rescaled and bias-corrected' (per methods.md) before calibration computation, ensuring calibration measures reflect genuine confidence-accuracy relationships"

**2. No Acknowledgment of Test-Session Effects on Calibration**
- **Missing Content:** Calibration metrics aggregate across 4 test sessions (Days 0, 1, 3, 6) but no mention of how test session might interact with individual differences
- **Why It Matters:** Some participants might show better calibration on later tests due to learning, while others might show worse calibration due to forgetting, creating individual difference patterns unrelated to cognitive abilities
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "Are you predicting stable calibration traits or session-dependent calibration?"
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 6: Data Source - clarify that calibration metrics represent stable individual differences averaged across all test sessions, controlling for session-specific effects"
