#### Omission Errors (Missing Context or Claims)

**1. No Discussion of DASS Intercorrelations**
- **Missing Content:** DASS subscales are typically moderately correlated (r = 0.5-0.7), which could create multicollinearity issues
- **Why It Matters:** High intercorrelations may make it difficult to identify unique predictive effects of each subscale
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Analysis Approach - discuss VIF monitoring and potential need for composite DASS score if multicollinearity is problematic"

**2. Missing Discussion of Range Restriction**
- **Missing Content:** Subclinical sample may have restricted range on DASS scores, attenuating correlations
- **Why It Matters:** Range restriction could explain null findings independent of true absence of effects
- **Strength:** MODERATE  
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to interpretation guidelines - discuss how range restriction in healthy sample may limit detectability of effects"

**3. No Consideration of Test Anxiety**
- **Missing Content:** The VR testing situation itself may induce anxiety, potentially confounding anxiety-memory relationships
- **Why It Matters:** State anxiety during testing could interact with trait anxiety measures
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Addition:** "Acknowledge potential state-trait anxiety interaction in limitations or interpretation section"
