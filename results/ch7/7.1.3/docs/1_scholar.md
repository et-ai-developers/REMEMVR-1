- **Scholarly Criticism:** Presents working memory model as unchanged since 1992, ignoring 50+ years of refinements and criticisms
- **Counterevidence:** Hitch, Allen, & Baddeley (2025) 50th anniversary review documents substantial model evolution, particularly regarding central executive non-unitarity and episodic buffer integration
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge model development: 'Baddeley's Working Memory Model (1992, refined through 2025) posits dissociable verbal and visuospatial subsystems, with recent refinements clarifying central executive functions and episodic buffer integration.'"

**2. Oversimplified Temporal Memory Characterization**
- **Location:** 1_concept.md - Section 3: Hypothesis

- **Strength:** MODERATE

#### Omission Errors (Missing Context or Claims)

- **Missing Content:** Concept.md doesn't acknowledge 4-session repeated testing could confound forgetting with practice effects
- **Why It Matters:** IRT theta scores may improve due to familiarity despite genuine memory decay, potentially masking domain differences
- **Supporting Literature:** Bergman et al. (2024) demonstrated significant practice effects in repeated digital cognitive assessment even with multi-day delays
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you distinguish domain-specific memory decay from domain-specific practice effects across 4 test sessions?"
- **Strength:** CRITICAL
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 5: Analysis Approach - acknowledge IRT theta scoring advantages for practice effects (separates item difficulty from person ability) and discuss why domain-specific practice effects are unlikely to confound domain-specific decay patterns"

- **Why It Matters:** Spatial VR interaction during encoding could enhance spatial memory encoding quality, creating apparent domain differences unrelated to verbal/visuospatial retrieval systems
