#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Recognition Process Oversimplification**
- **Location:** Theoretical Background - Dual-Process Theory application
- **Claim Made:** "Recognition relies more on familiarity-based processes" while Free Recall requires recollection
- **Scholarly Criticism:** This oversimplifies dual-process theory - Recognition can heavily involve recollection, especially for detailed episodic memories in rich VR contexts
- **Counterevidence:** Yonelinas (2002) explicitly notes that Recognition involves both familiarity and recollection processes, with recollection contribution varying by task demands
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge that Recognition involves both processes but maintain that the relative balance differs - Free Recall requires primarily recollection while Recognition can rely more heavily on familiarity when available"

**2. Assumed Linear Relationship Between Process Similarity and Correlation Strength**
- **Location:** Theoretical Predictions section

- **Strength:** MINOR
