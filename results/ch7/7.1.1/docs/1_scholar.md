- **Supporting Literature:** Frontiers systematic review (2024) identified practice effects as a major confound in VR memory research, particularly in repeated-measures designs
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you distinguish genuine individual differences in memory ability from differential practice effects across participants?"
- **Strength:** CRITICAL
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Section 4: Analysis Approach - acknowledge that theta scores represent practice-adjusted ability estimates via IRT modeling, which partially controls for session effects but may still affect validity correlations."
