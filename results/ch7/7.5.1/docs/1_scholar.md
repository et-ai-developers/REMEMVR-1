#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Overstated Sleep Effects in Young Healthy Adults**
- **Location:** Section 2: Theoretical Background - Sleep Consolidation Theory paragraph
- **Claim Made:** "Adequate sleep supports memory consolidation processes, particularly hippocampal-dependent episodic memories"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** The concept appropriately qualifies that sleep effects may be attenuated in young healthy samples, but the theoretical development could better acknowledge that individual differences in sleep duration within normal ranges may have minimal impact on episodic memory performance
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "The qualification that 'effect may be small in young healthy sample' appropriately acknowledges this limitation. The inclusion of sleep as a predictor tests the boundary conditions of sleep-memory relationships in optimal populations."

**2. Simplified VR Familiarity Mechanism**
- **Location:** Section 2: Theoretical Background - Novelty and Familiarity Effects
- **Claim Made:** "Prior VR experience may reduce cognitive load from novel interface, improving task-relevant memory performance"
- **Scholarly Criticism:** This mechanism assumes that VR familiarity uniformly reduces cognitive load, but could also introduce different strategy use or attention patterns that might not uniformly benefit performance
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "The predicted weak effect (Beta = 0.05-0.15) appropriately reflects uncertainty about VR familiarity mechanisms. The inclusion as an exploratory predictor allows empirical testing of competing predictions."
