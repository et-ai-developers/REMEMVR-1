#### Omission Errors (Missing Context or Claims)

**1. No Discussion of Practice Effects from Repeated Testing**
- **Missing Content:** Concept doesn't acknowledge that REMEMVR scores represent mean across 4 testing sessions (T1-T4)
- **Why It Matters:** Practice effects across sessions could inflate REMEMVR relative to single-session RAVLT, artificially creating VR-favored discrepancies
- **Supporting Evidence:** Methods indicates 4 timepoints (Days 0,1,3,6) for REMEMVR vs single-session RAVLT
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "Could practice effects explain VR-favored patterns rather than environmental scaffolding?"
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Analysis Approach - acknowledge that theta scores represent mean across sessions, discuss whether practice effects are controlled in Ch5 theta estimation, or note as limitation"

**2. Missing Power Analysis Details for Group Comparisons**
- **Missing Content:** Expected group sizes mentioned (n≈16 per extreme group) but no formal power analysis
- **Why It Matters:** With potentially small effect sizes for demographic differences, power considerations critical for interpretation
- **Supporting Evidence:** Success criteria mentions power ≥0.80 but no calculations provided
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "Is sample adequate to detect clinically meaningful age differences?"
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Addition:** "Include formal power analysis for detecting medium effects (d=0.5) with expected group sizes in Analysis Approach section"

#### Alternative Theoretical Frameworks (Not Considered)

**1. Measurement Error/Reliability Differences**
- **Alternative Theory:** Discrepancy patterns might reflect differential measurement reliability between RAVLT (established) and REMEMVR (novel) rather than true ability differences
- **How It Applies:** Lower reliability in REMEMVR could create artificial discrepancies, particularly in older adults who might be more variable on novel tasks
- **Key Consideration:** RAVLT has decades of psychometric validation while REMEMVR is experimental
- **Why Concept Should Address It:** Reviewers will question whether discrepancies reflect real differences vs measurement artifacts
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Acknowledgment:** "Acknowledge measurement reliability differences as alternative explanation, note that IRT theta scores (Ch5) provide reliability information for REMEMVR component"

**2. Technology Familiarity Confound**
- **Alternative Theory:** VR-favored patterns might reflect technology familiarity rather than environmental scaffolding benefits
- **How It Applies:** Younger adults with greater VR experience might paradoxically show poorer REMEMVR performance due to overconfidence, while older adults approach VR more systematically
- **Key Consideration:** Methods indicates VR experience collected as demographic variable
- **Why Concept Should Address It:** Age effects could be confounded with technology experience
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Acknowledgment:** "Note that VR experience will be examined as covariate to separate age effects from technology familiarity effects"

- **How It Could Affect Results:** Different standardization approaches could create artificial discrepancies not reflecting true ability differences
- **Literature Evidence:** Standard neuropsychological practice uses age-corrected norms for RAVLT
- **Why Relevant to This RQ:** Discrepancy calculation requires comparable standardization approaches
- **Strength:** MODERATE

- **Confound Description:** RAVLT administered during Day 0 session while REMEMVR represents performance across Days 0,1,3,6

- **Why Relevant to This RQ:** Different temporal sampling could confound ability comparison
- **Strength:** MINOR
