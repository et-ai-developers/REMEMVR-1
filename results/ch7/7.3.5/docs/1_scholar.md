#### Commission Errors (Critiques of Claims Made)

**1. Assumption of Unidirectional Causality**
- **Location:** Hypothesis section - "metacognitive awareness as a cognitive reserve indicator"
- **Claim Made:** Well-calibrated individuals have higher cognitive reserve (reserve → calibration)
- **Scholarly Criticism:** The direction of causality may be reversed or bidirectional - good performance may lead to higher confidence rather than reserve protecting calibration
- **Counterevidence:** Based on general metacognitive literature showing performance influences confidence judgments
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge bidirectional relationship possibility. Frame as correlational analysis rather than causal. Consider longitudinal designs for future work to establish temporal precedence."

**2. Conflation of Different Reserve Indicators**
- **Location:** Analysis Approach - education, RPM, and age as equivalent reserve measures
- **Claim Made:** Education and RPM are treated as equivalent cognitive reserve indicators
- **Scholarly Criticism:** Education (crystallized reserve) and fluid intelligence (processing capacity) may have different relationships with metacognitive calibration
- **Counterevidence:** Established differentiation between crystallized and fluid intelligence effects on cognition
- **Strength:** MINOR
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** "Acknowledge different types of reserve. Analyze education and RPM separately before combining. Discuss differential predictions for crystallized vs. fluid abilities."

#### Omission Errors (Missing Context or Claims)

**1. No Discussion of Confidence Scale Limitations**
- **Missing Content:** Concept.md doesn't address the 5-point Likert scale limitations for calibration analysis
- **Why It Matters:** Ceiling/floor effects and scale granularity could affect calibration quality measures, particularly for high-performing individuals
- **Supporting Literature:** General psychometric principles regarding scale sensitivity
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you ensure sufficient sensitivity in confidence measurements to detect meaningful calibration differences?"
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add to Analysis Approach - discuss confidence scale validation, potential rescaling approaches, and sensitivity analysis for scale effects"

**2. Missing Age-Related Calibration Discussion**
- **Missing Content:** No discussion of how aging affects calibration independent of reserve
- **Why It Matters:** Age-related changes in metacognitive monitoring could confound reserve effects, especially given 20-70 age range
- **Supporting Literature:** Established aging effects on metacognitive monitoring accuracy
- **Potential Reviewer Question:** "How do you separate normal aging effects on calibration from reserve-protective effects?"
- **Strength:** CRITICAL
- **Suggested Addition:** "Add age as covariate discussion in Analysis Approach. Consider age×calibration interactions. Acknowledge aging confound in limitations."

**3. No Baseline Performance Equating Discussion**

- **Why It Matters:** If well-calibrated group simply performs better, reserve indicators might reflect performance rather than calibration quality
