- **Scholarly Criticism:** These expectations may be optimistic for cross-validated performance in a 100-participant sample, particularly given the complexity of episodic memory and potential noise in VR-based measures
- **Counterevidence:** Cognitive prediction models typically show substantial shrinkage from training to cross-validation, and VR measures may have additional measurement error
- **Strength:** MODERATE
- **Suggested Rebuttal:** Consider more conservative CV-R² expectations (0.20-0.30) or provide justification for higher expectations based on pilot data or theoretical considerations
