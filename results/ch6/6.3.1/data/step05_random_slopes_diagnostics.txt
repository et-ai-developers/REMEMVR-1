QUESTION: Do individual forgetting rates vary (heterogeneous effects)?
OR are all participants declining at similar rates (homogeneous effects)?

METHOD: Compare intercepts-only vs intercepts+slopes LMM via AIC

  - Intercepts+slopes: Both baselines AND decline rates vary by individual

DECISION: SLOPES IMPROVE FIT - Individual differences confirmed

Random slopes model preferred (ΔAIC > 2). Individual forgetting rates vary.

IMPLICATION FOR THESIS:
  - Confidence decline rates are HETEROGENEOUS across participants
  - Some individuals forget faster, others slower
  - Domain × Time interaction reflects AVERAGE effect, but individual variation exists
  - Future work: Cluster participants by decline trajectories

REFERENCE:
